## A semantics bug the first regeneration exposed
The generator initially declared every zero-leaf target as an exclusion, which
made 35 targets that DO execute (crates with no #[test], reporting zero cases)
look like "declared excluded but ran anyway" - 76 anomalies. Two different
things wear the same "zero leaves" label: a [[bench]] target is compiled and
never executed as a test, so it produces no row; a crate with no #[test] is
executed and reports zero cases, so it stays required and is simply not
case-bearing. Only the first kind is exempt from the denominator now.
