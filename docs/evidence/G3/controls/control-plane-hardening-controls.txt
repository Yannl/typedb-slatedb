## Q-12 / 12.6 - the iteration cut is SERVER-owned
Opening an iterator returned only a head, and every subsequent scan page
accepted a caller-supplied `throughLsn`. That is not a pinned view: the
client held the cut, so it could widen it between pages (observing appends
made after iteration started, inv. 41-42) or narrow it (silently skipping
records a consumer believes it replayed). The iterator now returns an opaque
snapshot id MACed over (database, generation, head, incarnation), the scan
route refuses a caller-supplied bound outright, and a continuation outside
its own snapshot is typed rather than an empty page.
